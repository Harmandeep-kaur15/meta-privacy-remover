About MetaGuard

MetaGuard is a privacy-first metadata scanner that helps protect your images, videos, and documents.

Hidden metadata can reveal location, device details, and timestamps. MetaGuard helps you inspect and remove that data before sharing.

  • Local-first scanning
  • Easy file cleanup
  • Privacy guidance and reporting

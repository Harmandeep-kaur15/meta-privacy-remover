About MetaGuard

MetaGuard helps protect your privacy by removing hidden metadata from your files.

Every photo, video, and document you create contains hidden information that can reveal where you took a photo, what device you used, and when. This information can be misused for tracking or identity theft.

Why MetaGuard? Metadata removal is critical for journalists, activists, remote workers, and everyday users who share files online. We make privacy protection simple and accessible to everyone.

  • ✓ Removes GPS location data from photos
  • ✓ Strips device and camera information
  • ✓ Cleans timestamps and creation dates
  • ✓ Processes videos, PDFs, and Office documents
  • ✓ All processing happens on your device—files never leave
  • ✓ Free and privacy-first

Real-world use cases:

  • Social media: Remove metadata before uploading to Instagram, Twitter, or TikTok
  • Professional: Protect client documents and confidential communications
  • Safety: Help activists and journalists protect their location
  • General privacy: Maintain control over your digital footprint

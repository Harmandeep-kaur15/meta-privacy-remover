Privacy Guide

MetaGuard helps you understand privacy risks and keep your files safe.

Remove Location Data

Hidden GPS coordinates can reveal where photos were taken. Always clean location metadata before sharing.

Strip Device Info

Camera make, model, and device identifiers are often stored in files. Remove them to preserve anonymity.

Clear Timestamps

Creation and edit dates can expose your schedule. Remove or reset timestamps before you publish files.

Awareness through images and file data

Photos contain hidden history

Images often include camera settings, GPS coordinates, device identifiers, and editing software details.

Documents leak author metadata

Office files can preserve author names, revision comments, and hidden notes that reveal personal or business details.

Videos and archives hide tags

Video and multimedia files may carry encoded tags, timestamps, and GPS markers that are easy to miss.

Stay aware by checking for:

  • GPS coordinates embedded in photo files
  • Device and camera model metadata
  • Hidden author names and revision history
  • File creation and modification timestamps
  • Embedded thumbnails or hidden image layers

How to protect your data

  • Scan every file before sharing to identify hidden metadata.
  • Use MetaGuard's clean tools to remove metadata from images, documents and videos.
  • Review privacy settings on apps before uploading files.
  • Keep original files private and only share cleaned copies.
